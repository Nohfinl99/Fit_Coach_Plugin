## Evidence key

- **E1 — ACSM Position Stand 2026.** Currier et al., *Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults: An Overview of Reviews*, 137 systematic reviews, >30,000 participants, healthy adults ≥18; underlying searches through October 2024. Full article inspected. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/).
- **E2 — Pelland et al. 2026.** Meta-regressions of 67 studies, 2,058 participants (79.1% male; mean age 25.16), classifying direct/indirect sets and examining volume/frequency. PubMed abstract inspected; full paper not audited. Volume slope positive with diminishing returns; hypertrophy frequency effect compatible with negligible effect; strength frequency effect positive with diminishing returns. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41343037/).
- **E3 — Refalo et al. 2023.** Systematic review/meta-analysis of proximity to failure and hypertrophy; full PMC article inspected. Reaching momentary failure was not superior in subgroup analysis; exact optimal proximity could not be identified. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9935748/).
- **E4 — Vietnamese older-adult intervention (2026).** Doan et al., 90 community-dwelling adults aged 60–70 with AWGS-defined sarcopenia in Can Gio; 24-week aerobic + resistance exercise, initially supervised then home-based; one community per randomized arm, so community and treatment are confounded. PubMed abstract inspected. It reports grip-strength improvement versus control and modest muscle-mass changes, but cannot establish definitive treatment effects or isolate nutritional support. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42797063/).
- **E5 — Vietnamese population context.** WHO/MOH Viet Nam STEPS 2021 is population surveillance, not a resistance-training dose trial; landing page/metadata inspected in prior scan. [WHO record](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789290620266).

## Product-level implications

1. Treat the imported protocol table as retrieval content that can conflict with the evidence overlay. A prose warning alone is insufficient: benchmark retrieval tests must challenge a legacy rule and verify that the model refuses to present it as established individualized advice.
2. Preserve the source revision, exact row IDs and statuses. This is a proposed use restriction, not permission to edit the GitHub repo or alter historical archive bytes.
3. High-model review checks logic and coverage, not domain correctness. Qualified strength, medical and nutrition reviewers still need to adjudicate their relevant cases.
4. The current product test suite's `NOT_RUN` state remains unchanged; the audit used static source inspection and external literature only.

## Known gaps

- Full paper-level review for E2, the full Vietnamese trial methods/protocol and relevant Vietnamese guidance is still needed before those claims can be “approved.”
- No representative Vietnamese gym-user needs/outcomes survey was verified. Community forum posts remain self-selected discovery leads.
- No prospective Fit_Coach outcome study or actual-host behavior test was run.
- Nutrition, injury management, pregnancy, youth, clinical conditions and competition programming are outside this audit's validated scope.
