## Role-specific scope inserts

- **Strength reviewer:** Review item-level programming claims, exercise selection/cues, volume/intensity/progression examples, calibration anchors and general-population applicability. Do not review/approve medical diagnosis, rehabilitation, or nutrition unless separately qualified and assigned.
- **Medical reviewer:** Review acute exercise red flags, stop/urgent/emergency actions, medical referral boundaries and rapid weight-cut/dehydration refusal language within current clinical scope. Do not approve programming efficacy, diagnosis by the model, or rehabilitation plans.
- **Musculoskeletal clinician:** Review pain/injury escalation and warning-sign distinctions. V1 does not diagnose or prescribe rehabilitation.
- **Nutrition reviewer:** Review only retained food/nutrition/supplement claims and cases within documented nutrition qualification. If no qualified reviewer is engaged, those advice areas are removed from V1; rapid-cut/refusal safety review remains separately assigned.

## Owner checkpoints for inquiry and commissioning

- **Before an initial inquiry:** select the person and role from the current shortlist; resolve identity ambiguity; obtain the project owner's explicit authorization to contact and select the channel. Do not use personal contact details unless publicly provided for professional inquiries or supplied/approved by the owner. The initial inquiry may ask whether the person is interested and available, but must not imply a commission.
- **Before commissioning or sharing the packet:** agree in writing on scope, fee, expected time, deadline, confidentiality, internal record storage, attribution/public quotation, and allowed use of comments.
- Share only the exact packet/revision and minimum source materials needed for that role; do not send end-user data.
- Store responses, qualification evidence, fee terms, and contact details outside the public repository with access limited to the project owner/review process. Record consent status separately for internal review and public attribution. Silence or non-response is not a review and does not close FC-03.
- Keep FC-03 open until the required independent domain reviews and decision records in `domain-review-packet-v0.3.md` are complete and separately reviewed.
