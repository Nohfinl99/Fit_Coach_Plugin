# Fit_Coach interview-conversation benchmark research v0.1

**Status:** Desk source scan / candidate cases only / NOT_R4a / NOT_RUN  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-02  
**Purpose:** Find public, long-form interview or multi-speaker gym/training conversations on large distribution platforms and convert them into bounded candidate benchmark scenarios for later domain review. This document does not reproduce transcripts, validate interview claims, run Fit_Coach, close FC-03, or authorize R4a.

## Executive result

The most usable source mix found is:

1. Two Huberman Lab expert interviews with public transcripts and distribution across YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts: Layne Norton (2024) and Andy Galpin (2022).
2. A Stronger by Science interview with Eric Helms (2019), whose official episode page links Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube and provides topic timestamps.
3. Two VOV2 reports with actual Vietnamese gym-user/coach/clinician voices: an overtraining/rhabdomyolysis report (2025) and a supplement-marketing investigation (2024-10-31).
4. A recent Layne Norton–Jeremy Loenneke interview on blood-flow-restriction training, listed on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts by the official BioLayne page, which also supplies chapter timestamps. Keep it as a source lead only: dialogue was not independently reviewed for this scan.

These sources are useful for testing conversational failure modes: host-versus-guest attribution, confident numeric framing, disagreement/qualification, evidence-to-individual transfer, safety escalation, supplement claims, and Vietnamese language/context. The interviews themselves are **stimulus material, not ground truth**. A candidate benchmark answer must be checked against suitable independent evidence and qualified reviewers before any calibration or scoring.

## Recommended use as a benchmark dataset

1. Keep this as a **source-derived challenge set**, separate from the existing calibration candidate. Each case links to an interview locator but asks a newly written question; do not store or redistribute full transcripts.
2. For every accepted case, store: source URL, speaker/turn attribution, timestamp or article-body topic locator, access date, transcript status, original synthetic prompt, intended population, case-level pass/fail conditions, independent evidence references, reviewer qualification/COI, and exact revision digest.
3. Default model input is only the synthetic user paraphrase; do not ask it to verify an interview it has not heard or to identify the exact original speaker. A separate source-attribution track would need a rights-reviewed, pinned evidence bundle supplied to the model and a separate oracle. When a model is eventually run as Fit_Coach, score it against the accepted case oracle, not against whether it agrees with the interview guest.
4. Keep Vietnam-specific scenarios in Vietnamese and metric units. Vietnamese context informs language, foods, care routing, schedule, cost, and equipment; nationality must not be used to infer physiology, safe training dose, metabolism, or body type.
5. Before calibration: qualified strength coaches review ICB-01–03; a qualified medical reviewer reviews ICB-04; a qualified nutrition professional must review ICB-05 if retained. ICB-06 needs transcript access plus scope review. Prompts are drafted in Vietnamese, but Vietnamese-language and cultural validity have not been independently reviewed. Preserve disagreement and adjudication records.
6. The current product packet requires accepted FC-03 and a separately reviewed FC-04 local candidate before calibration R4a. This scan is not that freeze: **no cases are frozen, no Fit_Coach outputs were generated, and no score/benchmark result exists**.

## Research limits and next atomic task

- No video/audio was downloaded, no transcript corpus was copied, no comments were sampled, and no product was run. Huberman transcript pages caution that the transcript may be under human review; VOV2 pieces are edited reports; platform availability may change.
