## Recommended selection order

1. For strength programming, first verify the two practical leads from separate organizations (Bùi Duy Dũng / Clavis and Coach Tấn / STEEL) for current certificates, adult recreational client experience, conflicts, and availability. They remain unverified leads until those checks are done. The USH lead is an academic reviewer option; the UPES1 lead is an alternate methods reviewer with an explicit athlete-population caveat. Do not present any pair as qualified/independent until the checks pass.
2. For medical safety, choose one currently licensed sports-medicine physician and confirm the exact scope. A separate musculoskeletal clinician is preferred unless one person documents both scopes.
3. Keep nutrition, food plans, supplement recommendations, and related benchmark cases out of V1 unless a currently qualified nutrition professional accepts the scope. If omitted, retain only medically reviewed acute-risk/refusal boundaries described in packet v0.3.
4. Maintain reviewer independence: disclose employment, financial, product, and referral relationships; do not count two reviewers from the same supervisory chain as independent. Preserve disagreements and use the packet's adjudication rule.

## Population and evidence guardrails

- “Vietnamese user” is a localization context, not a physiological category. Do not infer body type, sex-specific response, safe dose, calorie needs, or progression from nationality, ethnicity, or presumed body size.
- Ask reviewers to distinguish healthy adult recreational trainees from elite athletes, students, clinical patients, adolescents, and older adults. Evidence for one group does not automatically validate advice for another.
- Ask reviewers to state what data the product must collect before giving individualized advice and when it should abstain or refer. Do not convert their review into claims of proven effectiveness or safety.
- Public-source screening includes institutional pages, professional journal/authority pages, and coach/employer profiles. Coach qualifications on commercial profiles are not independent verification. No direct credential registry, reference check, interview, consent, current availability, price, or conflict check was performed.
